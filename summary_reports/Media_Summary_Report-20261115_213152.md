 The text is about the Ethical Artificial Intelligence (AI) Framework and Guideline in Hong Kong, which aims to ensure safe and orderly AI application in various organizations and enterprises. The Digital Policy Office (DPO) is working on sector-specific AI application guidelines, accreditation, and governance frameworks. In 2027, the DPO will promulgate AI Agent safety-management guidelines.

The Government will launch the Digital Transformation Support Pilot Programme 2.0 to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) apply off-the-shelf basic AI digital solutions to enhance operational efficiency and market competitiveness. All AI solutions must comply with standards on data processing, cybersecurity, and related requirements.

The Government launched the "AI for All" Inclusive Programme to promote AI literacy across all sectors of society. The programme is being implemented through collaboration with three organizations, namely Cyberport, the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTPC), and the Hong Kong Productivity Council (HKPC).

In response to a Legislative Council question about supporting youth employment in the era of AI, the Government stated that it attaches great importance to promoting youth employment and development. The Government is conducting manpower projection exercises to assess the impact of AI on manpower demand and skill structures in the local labour market.

The Government will launch the two-year 30,000 Youth Employment and Internship Programme, which includes four employment and internship initiatives covering various sectors. The programme aims to provide young people with hands-on work experience, enhancing their competitiveness and adaptability in the workplace.

Overall, the text shows that the Hong Kong government is taking steps to ensure the safe and ethical application of AI, promote AI literacy, and support youth employment in the era of AI.
